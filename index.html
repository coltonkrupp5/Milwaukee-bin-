Questions people ask

How many bins do I need?

A rough guide: 20 for a studio, 30 for a one-bedroom, 45 for two bedrooms, 60 for three and 80 for four. If you have a lot of books or a packed garage, go one size up or add extra bins.

How does payment work?

A $50 refundable deposit reserves your bins and drop-off date. The full rental is due by your drop-off date. Your deposit is refunded once the bins are picked up with only normal moving wear.

I'm moving more than 30 minutes away. What does that cost?

There's a $60 trip charge for each drop-off or pick-up more than 30 minutes from Milwaukee. To avoid it, pick up or return the bins at our Wauwatosa location for free.

How early can you drop them off?

Most people want them 3 to 5 days before moving day. Your two weeks start on drop-off, so add a week if you like to pack early.

Do I need to be home for drop-off or pick-up?

No. Leave the empty bins stacked somewhere we can reach them, like a porch, garage or lobby, and text us when they're ready.

How much can I put in one bin?

Keep each bin to what you can lift safely, around 50 pounds. Heavy items like books go in the 17 gallon bins.

Do I have to clean the bins before you pick them up?

Just empty them. We wash every bin before it goes out again.

Can I use the bins for storage instead of a move?

Yes. Remodels, basement cleanouts and staging a home for sale all work. Book a package and add weeks as needed.
